# BizTrack Creator Guide

Welcome to BizTrack! As a Creator, you have full control over your Business Workspace.

## Getting Started
1. Sign up for an account.
2. You will be redirected to the Onboarding screen.
3. Enter your Business Name to provision your workspace.

## Customizing Your Workspace
Go to **Settings** (gear icon in the sidebar).
- **Business Template**: Change this to match your industry (e.g., Tuition, Gym, Workshop). The app will automatically rename "Students" to "Members" or "Clients".
- **Brand Color**: Select your primary color to brand the platform.
- **Modules**: Toggle features on or off. If you don't use Teachers, simply uncheck it. The app will hide it immediately to keep your dashboard clean.

## System Health
Under **Creator Settings -> Developer Tools**, you can access the System Health page to view active connections, database latency, and your underlying business IDs for support queries.
