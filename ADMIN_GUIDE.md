# BizTrack — Tuition Centre Administrator Guide

Welcome to **BizTrack**, the all-in-one management platform designed for tuition centres, academies, and education businesses.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Account Creation & Onboarding
1. Navigate to your BizTrack URL and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and a secure **Password**.
3. Upon first login, you will be prompted to create your business workspace (e.g. *Akademik Mega*).
4. Click **Get Started** — your workspace and default settings will be auto-configured.

---

## 👤 Managing Your Profile & Identity

### My Profile (`/profile`)
- **Name & Identity**: Click your avatar or initials badge in the top right corner and select **My Profile**.
- **Edit Details**: Update your Full Name, Phone Number, Job Title, Timezone (`Asia/Kuala_Lumpur`), and Language.
- **Instant Updates**: Click **Save Changes** — your name will immediately update across the Dashboard, Topbar greeting, and Sidebar profile chip without needing to log out.
- **Change Password**: Change your security password anytime under the **Change Password** section.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Hub

The Dashboard provides real-time visibility into your business health:

- **KPI Summary**:
  - **Active Students**: Count of enrolled active students.
  - **Active Teachers**: Staff members currently on directory.
  - **Classes**: Active learning groups and capacity.
  - **Monthly Revenue**: Total paid collections in current month (RM).
  - **Outstanding**: Total pending fees owed by students for the current month.
- **Revenue Overview Chart**: 6-month visual comparison of Income vs Operational Expenses.
- **Today's Focus**: Highlights collection progress and outstanding alerts.
- **Quick Actions**: One-click shortcuts to add students, record payments, create classes, or log expenses.

---

## 🎓 Directory & Operations Management

### 1. Managing Students (`/students`)
- **Add Student**: Click `+ Add Students`. Enter student name, contact info, assign to a class, and set the monthly fee in Ringgit (RM).
- **Search & Filter**: Use the top search bar to quickly search by student name, phone, email, or class name. Filter between *Active* and *Inactive* students.
- **Edit & Soft Delete**: Use the edit and trash icons on any student row. Soft-deleted students are hidden from active lists while preserving financial audit history.

### 2. Managing Teachers & Staff (`/teachers`)
- **Add Staff**: Log instructor names, roles, contact numbers, and compensation structure (Monthly, Hourly, or Commission).
- **Search**: Fast real-time name and role search.

### 3. Managing Classes & Groups (`/classes`)
- **Create Class**: Specify class name, assigned teacher, monthly fee rate (RM), and max student capacity.
- **Enrolment**: Assign students to their respective classes during student creation or editing.

---

## 💳 Finance, Payments & Expenses

### 1. Recording Student Payments (`/payments`)
- Click `+ Record Payment`.
- Select the Student, Payment Amount (RM), Date, Month, Year, and Payment Method (*Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Online*).
- Status can be marked as **Paid** or **Pending**.
- Paid collections automatically update monthly revenue metrics on the Dashboard and Financial Reports.

### 2. Logging Operational Expenses (`/expenses`)
- Click `+ Record Expense`.
- Select Date, Expense Category (*Rental, Utilities, Internet, Marketing, Salary, Equipment, Transport, Others*), Description, and Amount (RM).

### 3. Financial Reports & Printing (`/reports`)
- Select financial year, month, or class filters.
- Displays Total Income, Total Expenses, and Net Profit summary.
- Click **Print Report** for clean, browser-optimized A4 printing suitable for accounting audits.

## ⚙️ Customization & System Settings

### Creator Settings (`/settings`)
Available to workspace Creators and Admins:
- **Business Template**: Toggle between *Tuition Centre*, *Gym*, and *Workshop* to adjust terminology across the app.
- **Default Currency**: Set to `MYR (Malaysian Ringgit)`.
- **Enabled Modules**: Turn individual modules (Students, Teachers, Classes, Payments, Expenses, Reports) on or off according to your business needs.
